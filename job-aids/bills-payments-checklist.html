Bills and Vendor Payments Checklist

Use this quick-reference checklist during labs, capstone practice, and live workflow review. Complete training work only in a sandbox or training environment.

  • Create or import the bill.
  • Verify portfolio, property, GL code, amount, and due date.
  • Attach work order or invoice support when needed.
  • Approve the bill according to workflow.
  • Apply credits or markups/discounts when applicable.
  • Pay by check, ACH, NACHA, or remote check as appropriate.
