Move-Out and Renewal Checklist

Use this quick-reference checklist during labs, capstone practice, and live workflow review. Complete training work only in a sandbox or training environment.

  • Review lease expiration and eligibility.
  • Create renewal offer or start move-out.
  • For move-out, enter dates and forwarding details.
  • Review final charges, credits, deposit deductions, and refunds.
  • Finalize only after accounting is correct.
  • Confirm tenant ledger reaches the expected balance.
