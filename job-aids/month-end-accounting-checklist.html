Month-End Accounting Checklist

Use this quick-reference checklist during labs, capstone practice, and live workflow review. Complete training work only in a sandbox or training environment.

  • Confirm bank reconciliations are current.
  • Review diagnostics cards.
  • Resolve negative balances, reserve gaps, escrow mismatches, and prepayment mismatches.
  • Post management fees.
  • Prepare owner statements.
  • Export or save reports required by the company.
