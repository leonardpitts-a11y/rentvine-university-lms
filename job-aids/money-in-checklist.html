Money In Checklist

Use this quick-reference checklist during labs, capstone practice, and live workflow review. Complete training work only in a sandbox or training environment.

  • Record tenant receipts to the correct lease and bank account.
  • Record owner contributions when needed.
  • Group manual receipts into deposits.
  • Track electronic settlements.
  • Verify payment application order.
  • Confirm ledger balances after posting.
