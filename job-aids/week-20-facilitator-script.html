Financial Onboarding: The Customer-Facing Process

Purpose: This is a full trainer/facilitator script covering the four-step, customer-facing financial onboarding timeline: opening new trust bank accounts, reconciling existing accounts, the financial blackout period, and the financial balance forward. It's adapted from Rentvine's internal Financial Onboarding process documents and Dondre Brown's Aug 11, 2026 "Department Intro: Financial Onboarding" session. Use it to prepare for explaining this process to a new team member, a customer, or yourself while practicing. Lines marked "Suggested trainer language" are ready-to-use scripting you can say almost word for word.

Introduction

Today's session is a department introduction to Financial Onboarding. Before we get into the mechanics of the Balance Forward Workbook or any of the tools inside Rentvine, I want everyone to walk away understanding the process the way a customer actually experiences it -- the four steps, in order, and why each one exists.

Accounting is the backbone of the product experience. If a customer's starting balances are wrong, every owner statement, every reconciliation, and every report is wrong from day one. That's why we treat this as a structured, sequenced process rather than a flip-a-switch migration.

Why Financial Onboarding Comes First

A prospective customer will sometimes ask why they can't just sign up and start using Rentvine the same day. The honest answer is that accounting can't be migrated instantly and stay accurate -- it has to be sequenced.

We treat financial onboarding as a structured process, not a flip-a-switch migration, because if your starting balances are wrong, every owner statement and every reconciliation after that is wrong too.

Step 1: Open New Trust Bank Accounts

At the very start of onboarding, the customer opens new trust bank accounts to use inside Rentvine. These accounts can stay with the customer's current bank -- they don't need to switch banks -- but they need to start as a blank slate: no funding, no transactions, until we give the go-ahead.

The reason we recommend new accounts instead of carrying old ones forward is simple: NSFs and chargebacks can hit an old account up to 90 days later, and those are very difficult to trace once the switch is complete. Opening new accounts avoids cross-reconciling between two systems and keeps old problems from following the customer into Rentvine.

Go ahead and open the new trust accounts now, but don't fund them or record anything in them yet -- we'll tell you exactly when it's time.

Step 2: Reconcile Existing Bank Accounts

This step runs throughout onboarding, not at a single point in time. The customer keeps their existing accounts three-way reconciled -- bank, property/portfolio ledger, and general ledger all agreeing -- through the month prior to the cutoff date. A mid-month reconciliation through the cutoff date helps even more.

Clean historical records make everything downstream easier and faster. If the old software's books are already a mess going into the cutoff date, that mess follows the customer into their new balances.

Step 3: The Financial Blackout Period

Three days before the cutoff date, the customer shuts down incoming electronic payments in the old software -- including cash-pay slips. This is the Financial Blackout Period.

The reason it starts three days early rather than on the cutoff date itself is settlement time: shutting off incoming payments early gives nearly everything a chance to clear, so by the cutoff date the balance snapshot is as accurate as possible.

Three days before your cutoff date, go ahead and turn off incoming electronic payments in your old system -- that gives everything time to settle so your numbers on cutoff day are clean.

Step 4: Financial Balance Forward

On, or shortly after, the cutoff date, the Rentvine finance team pulls the financial balance reports from the customer's prior software, validates them, and tells the customer exactly how much to transfer into the new trust accounts. Once that's confirmed, the onboarding specialist enters the balances into Rentvine.

This is the step that Week 21's Balance Forward Workbook deep dive covers in full mechanical detail -- what migrates, what doesn't, and how the numbers actually get entered.

A Quick Preview: What Migrates and What Doesn't

Customers almost always ask what happens to their existing data. At a high level:

  • Migrates and affects the bank balance: security deposits, tenant prepayments, and uncleared items like outstanding checks or deposits.
  • Migrates but doesn't affect the bank balance: unpaid tenant charges, unpaid vendor bills, vendor credits, and owner/vendor 1099 income for the current tax year.
  • Does not migrate: detailed historical ledger/transaction history -- customers can keep that as an external file attachment inside Rentvine instead.

Week 21 covers exactly how each of these categories gets entered or imported.

Customer Sign-Off and Disclosures

Before anything is imported, the customer reviews and signs off on their compiled financial balances. This isn't just a formality -- it's what confirms the customer has checked the numbers themselves, and it clarifies that Rentvine isn't responsible for accounting errors that existed in the prior software.

Before we import anything, take a look at these compiled balances and confirm they're correct -- once you sign off, we'll bring them into Rentvine exactly as shown.

Typical Timeline

Start to finish, onboarding completion typically takes 1 to 7 business days, depending on account size, the quality of the customer's prior records, and how quickly the customer responds to requests. A small, clean account can move quickly; a large account with messy records or a slow-to-respond customer will take longer.

Common Customer Questions

"Why can't I just keep using my old bank account?" Because NSFs and chargebacks can surface up to 90 days later, and a new account avoids cross-reconciling two systems at once.

"Why do I have to stop taking payments before the cutoff date?" So the balance snapshot we pull on cutoff day is accurate -- most transactions will have settled by then.

"What happens to my old transaction history?" It doesn't migrate into Rentvine's ledgers, but it isn't lost -- it can be attached as a reference file at the portfolio or property level.

Closing

That's the customer-facing side of financial onboarding: new trust accounts, ongoing reconciliation, the blackout period, and the balance forward -- in that order, every time.
