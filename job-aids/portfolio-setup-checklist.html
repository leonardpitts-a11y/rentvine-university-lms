Portfolio Setup Checklist

Use this quick-reference checklist during labs, capstone practice, and live workflow review. Complete training work only in a sandbox or training environment.

  • Confirm owner legal entity and contacts.
  • Create or verify the portfolio.
  • Set ownership/distribution percentages.
  • Review reserve and distribution-hold settings.
  • Confirm statement and payout preferences.
  • Verify portfolio ledger and balances.
