Owner Statement Checklist

Use this quick-reference checklist during labs, capstone practice, and live workflow review. Complete training work only in a sandbox or training environment.

  • Review receipts, bills, fees, reserves, and distributions.
  • Check for negative balances and diagnostics issues.
  • Generate owner statement for the correct date range.
  • Review statement detail and supporting reports.
  • Publish or send statement.
  • Document any owner questions or corrections.
