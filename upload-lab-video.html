Lab Video Upload Center

This page provides the required lab-video submission workflow and receipt builder for the offline package.

Important This static ZIP package cannot send video files to a server by itself. Use your LMS, Google Drive, OneDrive, or form upload destination to submit the actual video, then paste the submitted video URL or confirmation number back into the module. Administrators can replace the default upload link in data/course.json and data/course-data.js with the real upload URL.
